ADMIN HANDOFF

Important safety notes

Never expose the secret key.
Only the Supabase publishable key belongs in js/config.js. Do not paste a service-role key into website files.

Real reviews only.
Customer reviews enter the queue as pending. Approve only authentic feedback, and highlight a review only with publication consent.

Table bookings are by phone.
Guests call the verified restaurant number shown on the public website. Keep the phone number and opening hours accurate in Website & branding.

IMAGE GUIDE

Good images make the story.

  • Use real restaurant photographs wherever possible.
  • Prefer WebP or high-quality JPEG for food photography.
  • Upload a transparent PNG/WebP for your logo.
  • Use a short, descriptive alt text for accessibility.
  • For a new hero image, upload it and choose Use as hero below.

The website-media bucket is public for displaying published images, but file uploads, changes and deletion require an authenticated administrator.
